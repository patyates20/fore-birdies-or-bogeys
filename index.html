Who's driving the cart

Two people, one cart, zero supervision.

We met in college, got engaged, got married in 1990, and raised three boys who now out-drive both of us. Somewhere in the middle of all that we started playing golf together — which is either the best thing you can do for a marriage or the fastest way to test one.

Now the house is quiet and the clubs are already in the trunk. Good resorts, three stars or better. The round, the room, the bourbon, the band in the corner. We tell you all of it.
